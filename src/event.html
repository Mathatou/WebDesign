Events

The main events featured on the home page. Click a date on the calendar to jump straight to it.

Fri 5 Dec

Christmas Market

Town Centre, until 24 Dec

Discover exotic food, drinks, and gifts at the annual Christmas Market in Saint-Gratien.

Tue 8 Dec

Concert

Saint-Gratien conservatory

Come listen to the Jazz concert at the Saint-Gratien conservatory. This concert is brought to you by the Saint-Gratien music association and is free for all.

Tue 9 Dec

Music Lesson

Saint-Gratien conservatory

Following the Jazz concert, students of the Saint-Gratien conservatory will give a music lesson to the public. Come and learn about music and instruments with our talented students. Then entry is 40€ for the lesson and the capacity is limited to 10 people.

Thu 10 Dec

Volleyball Tournament

City Hall

Come and watch the Saint-Gratien volleyball team compete in the final against the Enghien-Les-Bains team in the regional tournament.

Thu 18 Dec

Figaro's Wedding

Theatre of Saint-Gratien

Assist to the theatre piece "Figaro's Wedding" at the Theatre of Saint-Gratien. This is a classic comedy by Pierre Beaumarchais, performed by the local theatre company. Come and enjoy this delightful performance!

Thu 31 Dec

New Year's Eve

City Hall

Celebrate New Year's Eve with food, drinks, music, and fireworks lighting up the sky.
